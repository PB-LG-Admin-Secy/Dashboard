How to update the data

The MCs fill a Google Form. You do not enter weekly figures at all.

Upload the data workbook to Drive and open it as a Google Sheet.

Build the form from the build sheet, and point its responses at that same spreadsheet. It creates a tab called Form Responses 1.

Share the sheet as Anyone with the link · Viewer and paste the link into Connect sheet above.

Send each MC its own pre-filled form link, so the city is already set and cannot be picked wrongly.

That is it. Submissions land in the sheet and this board reshapes them on load — no script to run, nothing to copy across.

You keep only three tabs by hand: Tenders (one row per package), Norms (the required figure for each parameter, which is why the form asks the MC only for the actual) and Actions. The Data tab is historical seed — leave it alone.

New in this version. Tenders has two extra columns: ie (Independent Engineer status) and prev_status (stage at the last review, used for ‘Changes since last review’). A new optional tab Notices (city, stream, vendor, type, date, amount_lakh, status, notes) feeds the Vendor Health Card, and a tab Scoring (item, value) sets how vendors are scored. In the Data tab, a GVP row with metric Eliminated records permanent removals, and day rows may carry text such as Sunday or Rain as the value. The form can add an optional question GVPs eliminated and, for tenders, Independent Engineer.

History comes free. Every submission carries its week ending date, so a new week appears as a button in the Week ending bar, and once two weeks exist each city panel grows a ‘Since last week’ column and a trend line. Nothing is ever overwritten — a resubmission for the same week and figure simply replaces that one value.
