How to update the data

ULBs report through the Google Form. They pick their district, their ULB and the layer, then the status and the quantities. The review date is filled in by the sheet.

HQ keeps three tabs by hand: Layers (state position of the 24 layers), Actions and Scoring. Progress holds ULB-wise entries made by HQ; the form adds to it on load.

Positions carry forward. A ULB only reports what changed. Its last reported status for each layer stays until a newer entry arrives, and a resubmission replaces the earlier one.
